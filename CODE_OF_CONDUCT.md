# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, abridged)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MAdvanceboard a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience
level, nationality, or religion.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- No harassment, trolling, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Respect differing viewpoints; focus on the technical merit.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported via
https://github.com/master8848/MAdvanceboard/issues. Maintainers may
remove comments/commits or ban contributors who violate this code.
